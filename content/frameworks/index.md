# Frameworks

This folder contains synthetic framework pages distilled from the bibliography.

## Entries

- [Separation of Concerns](separation-of-concerns.md) - the discipline of separating concerns so systems can be analyzed and redesigned cleanly.
- [Productivity J-Curve](productivity-j-curve.md) - explains why measured productivity can dip during early adoption because intangible complements have to be built first.
- [Boundary Testing](boundary-testing.md) - explains how actors challenge, redraw, and capture value through contested boundaries.
- [Media as Extensions](media-as-extensions.md) - explains how media and interfaces extend human faculties and reorganize attention, participation, and scale.
- [Technologically Mediated Cognition](technologically-mediated-cognition.md) - combines McLuhan, Clark-Chalmers, Vygotsky, and Verbeek into one careful AI-interface concept.
- [Platforms](platforms.md) - explains platforms as cores with complements, interfaces, and governance, drawing on Gawer and Cusumano.
- [Regenerative Software](regenerative-software.md) - explains how AI makes intent, interfaces, and evaluations durable while implementations become disposable.
