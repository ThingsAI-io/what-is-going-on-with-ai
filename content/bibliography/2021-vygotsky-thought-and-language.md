# Executive Summary

Vygotsky's central insight is that speech begins in social life and becomes internalized into verbal thought. Words are not mere labels for already finished ideas; they are part of the formation of higher mental functions.

That makes the book especially useful for understanding how language-heavy AI systems can shape expression and thought over time. It does not say that language fully determines cognition. It does say that language is constitutive of how higher mental life develops.

## Relevance to This Repository

This source is valuable because it gives the project a disciplined way to discuss how repeated AI-assisted writing or dialogue might shape internal speech, phrasing, and the organization of thought without turning the claim into crude linguistic determinism.
