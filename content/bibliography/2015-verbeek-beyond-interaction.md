# Executive Summary

Verbeek argues that technologies should not be treated as neutral objects standing between pre-given humans and pre-given things. Technologies mediate human-world relations: they shape how people perceive, act, decide, and inhabit their environment.

The point is not that technology determines everything. The point is that design choices matter because technologies help structure the practices and experiences through which the world becomes accessible to us.

## Relevance to This Repository

This source is important for AI because interfaces are not just delivery mechanisms. They shape habits, choices, and the texture of interaction. Verbeek provides a careful vocabulary for that mediating role without collapsing into simplistic determinism.
