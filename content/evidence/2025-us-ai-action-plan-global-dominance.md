# US "Winning the Race": America's AI Action Plan Frames Global AI Dominance as State Policy

## What This Is

On July 23, 2025, the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy published **"Winning the Race: America's AI Action Plan"** — the congressionally-and-executively-mandated strategy deliverable required by [Executive Order 14179](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/removing-barriers-to-american-leadership-in-artificial-intelligence/) ("Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence," Jan 23, 2025). Its opening sentence states the policy: *"The United States is in a race to achieve global dominance in artificial intelligence."* Its operative claim is that the largest AI ecosystem sets the world's standards and collects the economic and military proceeds.

The plan's two most analytically significant moves for this repository concern exactly the elements the [territories model](../model/territories-of-the-ai-frontier.md) predicts a sovereign would make:

1. **Open-source and open-weight models receive explicit geostrategic endorsement.** The plan states they "could become global standards in some areas" and "have geostrategic value," and directs the federal government to create "a supportive environment for open models" so "America" has "leading open models founded on American values." This is the US state formally arming its own open-model pirates-in-chief.
2. **The export-and-containment posture.** Commerce and State are instructed to deliver "secure, full-stack AI export packages" — hardware, models, software, applications, standards — to allies, while the diplomacy section recommends "countering Chinese influence in international governance bodies" and strengthening AI-compute and semiconductor export-control enforcement.

## The Headline Numbers and Positions

- **~90+ federal policy actions** across three pillars: Accelerating Innovation, Building American AI Infrastructure, Leading in International Diplomacy and Security.
- **Open-weights pillar**: explicitly endorses open-source/open-weight AI; argues that governments and companies with sensitive data cannot use closed-vendor models, and that startups avoid "dependence on a closed model provider."
- **Infrastructure pillar**: "Build, Baby, Build!" — data centers, semiconductor fabs, energy; the plan claims AI is the first digital service to demand "vastly greater energy generation," and that America's grid has "stagnated since the 1970s while China has rapidly built out their grid."
- **Diplomacy/security pillar**: "prevent our adversaries from free-riding on our innovation and investment"; export the stack to friends and allies; plug export-control loopholes.

## Interpretation: The Sovereign Claims the Territory

Read through the [boundary-testing](../frameworks/boundary-testing.md) lens, the Action Plan is the **US state attempting to be the first mover in codifying the AI frontier before the firms' private settlements harden**. Its position is not neutral about the three territories: on the **corpus** it stays silent on copyright (the [copyright politics](../evidence/2026-zuckerberg-future-is-for-everyone-ai-manifesto.md) of training data are delegated to Congress and the courts); on the **weights** it picks a side — open weights are the American standard because exports of open models "could become global standards"; on **action/execution** it defers to agencies. The plan is thus the state drawing the boundary *around* the territory, then letting domestic pirates and champions fight over the interior.

The plan also completes an inversion the [Zuckerberg manifesto](../evidence/2026-zuckerberg-future-is-for-everyone-ai-manifesto.md) began: where the corporate letter argued open-up-training-data on competitiveness grounds, the federal plan converts open weights into a **sovereignty instrument** — the [productivity J-curve's](../frameworks/productivity-j-curve.md) lag cut short by executive action so the state can set the boundary while the courts still work through the training-data and CFAA case law ([LibGen discovery](../evidence/legal/2026-openai-copyright-md-l-chatgpt-logs-discovery.md), [Ziff Davis scraping rulings](../evidence/legal/2025-ziff-davis-v-openai-robots-txt-dmca.md)). This is the "pirate-sovereign rotation" of the [model piece](../model/territories-of-the-ai-frontier.md) playing out at the level of the state itself.

## Limits

- **Political document.** The plan states goals, not outcomes; most "recommended policy actions" awaited agency implementation, and the repo records none of their execution status.
- **Framing win-lose.** "Global dominance" language is a maximalist framing of a contested field; it should be read alongside the [EU AI Continent Action Plan](2025-eu-ai-continent-action-plan-sovereignty.md) and [China's AI+ Initiative](2025-china-ai-plus-action-open-source.md) for a three-sided view.
- **No enforcement evidence.** Whether the export packages, governance-body counters, and grid buildout materialized is outside this source's four corners.
- **Source type.** A policy plan, not a measurement of the AI transformation; its evidentiary value is in documenting state self-understanding and intended boundary-setting.
